Support

Contact Zami

Contact the Zami maintainers on GitHub for help with the app. GitHub issues are public. Keep private messages, screenshots containing personal data, account credentials and recovery keys out of issues.

Report abuse

Press and hold a message on your phone, or open its actions on a computer, then select Report message. This sends the report to your Matrix homeserver’s moderators. You can also open the sender’s profile and choose Block. In App Settings under Privacy & Safety, you can manage blocked users and enter phrases you want to hide from incoming messages in the iOS release.

For a private follow-up, contact the abuse team for your homeserver or the room’s moderators. To report a problem with Zami’s safety tools, contact the Zami maintainers without including the offending content.

Community guidelines

Use Zami to communicate lawfully and respect people’s privacy and consent. Harassment, credible threats, hateful abuse, sexual exploitation, nonconsensual intimate imagery, impersonation and spam are prohibited. Follow the rules of your homeserver and each room you join.

Delete your Matrix account

Open App Settings, choose Account, then Delete account. Password accounts can confirm the request in Zami. With delegated sign-in, Zami opens the homeserver’s account management page when available. If your provider does not expose deletion there, contact your homeserver’s account support team. Deletion is permanent and signing out does not delete your account.
