Zami

Zam, the Matrix client, as a native app for iPhone and Android.

Zami is Zam ported to Expo and React Native. It runs Zam's own code natively: rooms and spaces, threads, end-to-end encryption, voice and video calls, and plugins. As Zam changes, Zami follows.

Zami is in development, and isn't in the App Store or Google Play yet. You can build it from its source.

Signing in

Zami signs in to your Matrix account on your homeserver. Servers that use Matrix Authentication Service show this page as the app's homepage when you sign in with Zami. This site has no accounts and keeps nothing about you.
