Zami

Privacy policy

Your Matrix account

Zami is a Matrix client. Your selected homeserver manages your account, sign-in, rooms, messages, files and reports. Your homeserver and other homeservers that participate in your rooms process data according to their own policies. End-to-end encryption protects message content in encrypted rooms. Room membership and other routing information can still be visible to servers.

Zami stores credentials, encryption keys, preferences and cached conversations on your device to keep you signed in and make conversations available offline. Zami sends your account credentials to the homeserver or sign-in provider you select.

Optional notifications

If you enable notifications, Zami registers a device push token with your homeserver. The homeserver sends the Zami push gateway that token, your Matrix user ID, room and event identifiers, and notification counts when needed. Zami sends this information through Expo and Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver the alert and open the right conversation. Zami uses a generic message alert.

The gateway processes push requests as they arrive and does not maintain a notification database. Its application logs record delivery counts and error codes. Cloudflare hosts the gateway and processes requests and operational metadata. Turn off push in Zami’s notification settings or revoke notification permission in your device settings.

Calls, location and external services

Zami requests camera and microphone access for features you choose, including calls, recording voice messages and scanning a sign-in code. Calls may connect to the calling infrastructure configured by your homeserver. When you choose to send or share your location, it is sent to the room and may be visible to its members. Viewing a map can contact its tile provider. Opening an external link takes you to the linked provider.

Zami does not sell personal data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Deleting data

Use Delete account in Zami’s Account settings to request deletion from your homeserver. Your homeserver controls server-side deletion and retention. Federated servers and conversation participants may retain their own copies. Signing out removes the local session. Removing the app clears its local storage.

Contact Zami support for questions about data Zami handles.
